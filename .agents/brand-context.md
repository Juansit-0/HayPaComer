# Brand Context

> Working name: HayPaComer. The final name is decided in the brand-naming step of Phase 0.5.

## Brand

- **Name**: HayPaComer (working name)
- **Category**: Smart home cooking assistant / food-waste reduction (Java app + ESP32 sensors + kitchen scale)
- **Description**: A fridge that knows what is actually at home and answers "what can I cook right now?"
- **Stage**: Pre-launch (prototype, v1.0 in development)
- **Website**: N/A

## Audience

- **Primary Audience**: Shared households (roommates, students) and young families who stock a common fridge and cook at home several times a week.
- **Key Problem**: Nobody has a reliable, current, actionable view of the food at home, so food expires unseen, purchases get duplicated, and dinner decisions start from scratch every night.
- **Their Language**: "what's in the fridge?", "what's about to expire?", "is that enough?", "what can I cook tonight with what I have?", "who does this belong to?"

## Positioning

- **Differentiation**: The cheapest instrumented path to real answers: an affordable scale and ESP32 sensors measure grams and cold chain; food ownership is respected; concrete cook-now suggestions (5/15/30 minutes) work offline; it complements a normal fridge instead of competing with premium smart fridges.
- **Competitors**: Samsung Family Hub / AI Vision Inside, FridgeVision, SuperCook, DecayDock
- **Market Position**: Niche (accessible domestic hardware; shared and family households)

## Brand Personality

- **Personality Words**: practical, approachable, honest, curious
- **Tone**: casual and direct
- **Voice Admires**: none specified yet (define during brand-identity)

## Values & Mission

- **Core Values**: waste less, measurable honesty (grams are measured, safety comes from rules), household respect (ownership and permissions), resilience (works offline), simplicity
- **Mission**: Help shared and family households waste less food and decide what to cook tonight with what is actually in the fridge.

## Goals

- **Primary Goal**: Ship v1.0 with a professional brand, the full 7-phase roadmap, and a public portfolio repository.
- **Key Metrics**: all roadmap phases completed; demo acceptance criteria passing (door alert, measured consumption, guided cooking, verified substitutions, AI outage fallback, analytics); CI green with coverage >= 80% in the core; brand applied to README, repository, and UI.
