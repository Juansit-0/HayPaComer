HayPaComer 1.0.0 is the first complete release of the smart fridge: measured inventory in grams, food ownership, cold chain alerts and investigations, cook-now evaluation with safe substitutions, guided cooking with a scale copilot and hands-free voice, planning and budget, an agent layer whose writes wait for a person, and analytics of food rescued and money saved.

- Run it: see "Run locally" in the README; `AI_PROVIDER=offline` works without any key.
- Demo: `docs/demo.md`, with `scripts/demo/seed.py` and `scripts/demo/sensors.py`.
- Changes: `CHANGELOG.md`.
- Quality: more than 670 automated tests including Testcontainers, coverage gates of 80% in `domain`, `application`, and `agent`, and CI that also compiles the ESP32 firmware.
