# HayPaComer 1.1.0

Plan v2: Spanish first, values tuned from the database, expiry dates that make sense, a redesigned interface, and production deployment.

## Highlights

- **Spanish and English:** the whole interface, errors, alerts, and food names follow the person's language, saved per account; texts live in PostgreSQL.
- **Values in the database:** door alert, safe temperature, cold rules, at risk days, briefing hours, AI limits, and sign in limits are settings with ranges; each household tunes its own from the new Settings screen.
- **Expiry with logic:**
  - food without a date gets an estimate by where it is stored;
  - impossible dates (milk or meat a month away) are rejected with the reason;
  - opening food shortens its date;
  - a label photo proposes the printed date only when Java finds it possible.
- **Session that stays:** closing the tab no longer signs people out; refresh is shared safely across tabs.
- **Redesign:** a bento layout based on research of real food sites (SuperCook, Rappi, Cookpad, Éxito):
  - bigger controls and readable numbers;
  - a four step add food sheet;
  - a market budget with thousands separators and price per kilo;
  - an accessibility pass with axe in light and dark themes.
- **Deployment:** production Docker image, `prod` profile, Render blueprint with deploys after green checks, smoke checks of the live commit, and a runbook.

## Upgrade notes

- Flyway migrations `V21` to `V28` run at startup; no manual steps.
- New production variables: `DB_HOST`, `DB_PORT`, `DB_NAME`, `DB_USER`, `DB_PASSWORD`, `DB_SSLMODE`, `REDIS_URL`, `PORT` (see `docs/runbook.md`).
